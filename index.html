My Beautiful Hinata 🥹❤️

My love, I honestly don't know if words can completely explain how sorry I am, but I still want to try. I wanted to make this little place for you because there are some things I really want you to hear from my heart.

First of all, I'm sorry. Truly, deeply, and genuinely sorry. 🥺❤️ I know I hurt you, and I know that what I did wasn't right. I lied to you, and I understand that I gave you a reason to question my honesty and my intentions. You trusted me, and I should have protected that trust instead of doing something that could hurt you.

I won't make excuses for what I did. I won't try to make it seem smaller than it was, because I know it wasn't small to you. Your feelings matter to me, and knowing that I caused you pain honestly hurts me too.

I already told you the truth because you deserved the truth. But even though I eventually told you everything, I know that doesn't erase the fact that I wasn't honest from the beginning. And for that, my love, I'm genuinely sorry.

I wish I could go back to that moment and make a different choice. I wish I could erase the disappointment from your face and the hurt from your heart. But I know life doesn't work that way. We can't change yesterday, but we can decide what we do from today onwards.

And I want you to know something, Hinata... I don't want to lose you. 🥹❤️

You have become such an important part of my life. It's the little things about you that stay in my mind — our conversations, our jokes, the random moments, the times we laugh over things that probably wouldn't even make sense to anyone else.

I love the memories we've created together, and I want there to be so many more of them. More laughter. More stupid jokes. More late conversations. More pictures. More moments where we're just together and everything else doesn't matter.

I'm not perfect, Hinata. I know that. I've made mistakes, and I'll probably make mistakes again because I'm human. But I don't want to use that as an excuse. I want to learn. I want to grow. I want to become someone whose actions make you feel safe, loved, respected and valued.

I don't expect you to forget everything overnight. I don't expect one apology to suddenly make everything okay. If you're hurt, take your time. If you're angry, I'll understand. If you need space, I'll respect that.

But even through all of that, I want you to know that I'm here. And I mean that sincerely.

I want to be by your side — not only when everything is perfect, but also when life gets difficult. I want to be there when you're happy, when you're stressed, when you're celebrating, when you're doubting yourself, and even on the days when you don't feel like talking.

I want to be someone you can look beside you and know, "He's still here." ❤️

I want to stand beside you through the good days and the bad ones. I want to support your dreams, celebrate your victories, encourage you when things aren't going your way, and remind you of how amazing you are whenever you forget.

I can't promise that I'll never make another mistake. But I can promise that I'll keep trying to become better. I can promise to be more honest, more thoughtful and more intentional with your heart.

Because your heart isn't something I want to take for granted. And your love isn't something I want to treat casually.

So, Hinata... if there is still a little space in your heart for me, I would love the opportunity to earn your trust again. Not just with words, but with my actions.

Thank you for every beautiful moment you've given me. Thank you for the smiles, the laughter, the memories and even the lessons. You've made my life more beautiful simply by being a part of it.

I'm sorry for hurting you, my love. I'm sorry for disappointing you. And I'm sorry for giving you a reason to doubt me.

But most importantly, I want you to know that I care about you deeply, and I'm willing to be by your side — today, tomorrow, and through whatever life decides to throw at us. 🥹❤️

I don't know exactly what the future holds for us, but if you'll let me, I'd love to keep walking through it with you.

So, my beautiful Hinata... 🥹❤️
